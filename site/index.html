A quick field guide.

Two players, one game

Create a room, then send its link to a friend. Keep the host’s tab open and visible. Kirby needs to inhale an enemy and gain an ability, then press A to create a helper for player two.

Controls

Arrows / D-pad: move · B / Z: jump · Y / S: inhale or attack · A / X: create helper · X / A: special action · L / Q and R / W: guard · Enter: Start · Shift: Select. A standard Bluetooth or USB gamepad also works.

On a phone

Rotate to landscape and use both thumbs. Tap Sound to enable audio. Android can request fullscreen and landscape lock. On iPhone, rotate manually; Add to Home Screen gives the game more space.

Android connection trouble? In Android Settings > Apps > your browser > Permissions, allow Nearby devices / Local network, then retry. Android 17 and newer can block same-Wi-Fi multiplayer until this is allowed. Also allow local network access if your browser asks.

About rooms

The host streams the game and receives your controls directly over WebRTC. A public PeerJS service introduces the two browsers. Some mobile and restricted networks need a TURN relay.

Connection settings

Optional: use your own signaling server or TURN relay. These settings apply to the next room and stay in memory for this tab only.
